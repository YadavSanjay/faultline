# FX forwards & cross-currency swaps

*Used by trade 27 (3M USD/JPY forward) and trade 26 (5Y USD/TWD cross-currency swap).*

---

## In plain English

These trades are bets/hedges on **exchange rates**. The key idea behind both is **covered
interest parity (CIP)**: the fair forward exchange rate isn't a guess about the future — it's
pinned down by today's spot rate and the interest rates in the two currencies. If USD earns more
than JPY, the forward USD/JPY must adjust so you can't earn a free lunch by borrowing one
currency, converting, and lending the other.

- An **FX forward** locks in an exchange rate for a single future date. Its value today is the
  difference between the rate you locked (the **strike**) and the current fair forward, discounted
  back. Our USD/JPY forward is a **carry overlay** (buy USD).
- A **cross-currency swap** is a series of fixed-rate interest payments in two different
  currencies, plus an exchange of principal. Our USD/TWD swap **receives USD / pays TWD** and
  hedges the FX risk of a long-TWD ADR position. It is **seasoned** (already running), so its
  value reflects how the TWD has moved since inception.

Both are valued by discounting each leg on its own currency's curve and converting to USD at the
current spot. The dominant driver of the P&L is simply **where spot is now versus the contract
rate**.

## How they're priced

### FX forward (TRADE_27)

> **Quantitative detail.** For a long-USD forward (receive USD notional `N`, pay JPY `N·K`):
> `PV = N·DF_usd(T) − N·K·DF_jpy(T)/S`, where `K` is the strike (JPY per USD), `S` the current
> spot, `DF_usd` the SOFR discount factor and `DF_jpy` a flat-curve discount factor at the foreign
> rate. The **fair forward** is `F = S·DF_usd/DF_jpy` (CIP); the trade is in-the-money when the
> strike beats the fair forward. `fx_delta_1pct` is the PV change for a 1% spot move. Code:
> [`src/pricer/ql/fx/fx_pricer.py`](../../../src/pricer/ql/fx/fx_pricer.py) (`FxForwardPricer`).

### Cross-currency swap (TRADE_26)

> **Quantitative detail.** Each leg is valued as a fixed-rate cash-flow stream **plus notional
> redemption**, discounted on its own curve:
> `PV_usd = Σ N_usd·c_usd·τ_i·DF_usd(t_i) + N_usd·DF_usd(T)` and likewise for the TWD leg in TWD.
> The net USD value is `PV = PV_usd − PV_twd/S` (for receive-USD). The TWD notional is fixed at
> inception by the contract FX (`N_twd = N_usd × 30.00`). Past coupons are skipped (it's
> seasoned). Annual frequency, `Act/360` accruals, `UnitedStates.Settlement` calendar,
> ModifiedFollowing. Code:
> [`src/pricer/ql/fx/fx_pricer.py`](../../../src/pricer/ql/fx/fx_pricer.py)
> (`CrossCurrencySwapPricer`).

### The curves

- **USD leg:** the SOFR curve (see [01-sofr-multicurve.md](01-sofr-multicurve.md)).
- **Foreign leg:** a **flat-forward curve** at a single rate — JPY uses BOJ **TONAR**, TWD uses the
  **CBC policy rate** (2.00%, a hardcoded config constant updated manually on CBC board days).
  The free feeds only give an **overnight** JPY rate (TONAR) and a TWD **spot** fixing — there is no
  JPY-OIS strip or TWD forward-points feed to bootstrap a foreign *term structure* — so the foreign
  curve is flat and the **XCCY basis is assumed zero** (C2).
- **USD/TWD spot (B3):** taken from the **CBC NDF fixing** (`cbc_twd_ndf_fix.usdtwd_cbc`) — the
  official reference the non-deliverable TWD book actually settles against. This also fixed a
  staleness problem: Fed H.10's TWD stops around 2026-05-29, so at the then-live 2026-07-10 book date the
  old source was ~6 weeks stale (31.37) while the CBC fixing is current (~32.18), which alone moves
  the #26 mark ~+18% at that date.

## Limitations & assumptions

- **Cross-currency basis via a config hook, default 0 (C2)** — `fx.currencies.<ccy>.xccy_basis_bp`
  is added to the foreign discount rate (`effective_foreign_rate = foreign_rate + basis/1e4`) and
  flows to both the CCS mark and the FX forward's fair forward. It defaults to 0 because there is
  **no free daily USD/TWD or USD/JPY xccy-basis feed**; real USD/TWD forwards can deviate 50–200 bp
  from CIP, so this basis risk is not captured until a licensed basis is dropped into config (no
  code change). `xccy_basis_bp` is reported in the diagnostics.
- **Flat foreign curves** — single TONAR / CBC rate, parallel-shifted in stress; no foreign term
  structure.
- **TWD rate is a manually-maintained constant** (CBC moves ~4×/year).
- **TRADE_27 economics are representative**; TRADE_26 is a defined seasoned trade hedging the TSM
  ADR (#2).
